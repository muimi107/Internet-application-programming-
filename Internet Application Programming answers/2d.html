Question 2(d): The alt Attribute

Importance

The alt attribute gives a text description of an image. It is shown when the image cannot load, it is read aloud by screen readers, and it helps search engines understand the image.

Example: <img src="clinic.jpg" alt="Nurse vaccinating a child at a Nairobi clinic">

Four consequences of 80% of images missing alt text

  1. Blind and low-vision users are excluded. A screen reader only says "image", so they miss vaccination schedules, charts and health posters.
  2. Users with slow internet or limited data lose information. When images fail to load, they see empty boxes instead of a description.
  3. Search engines cannot understand the images, so the site ranks lower and fewer people find the health information.
  4. The site fails accessibility standards and loses public trust. A user who cannot understand an important health image may also act on wrong information.
